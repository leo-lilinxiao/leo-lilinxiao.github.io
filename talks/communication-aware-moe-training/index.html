Inside the training system

CARD Communication-aware MoE training

Scheduling the work of a training step.

Frontiers Series · Guangming Laboratory · 3 June 2026

The idea, in brief

In this talk, I describe CARD, our approach to improving MoE training on Ascend NPU clusters. We use training logs and profiling to find where waiting and repeated computation slow a training step.

A closer look

Four optimization areas

  1. 01

    Communication

    Coordinate communication and computation

    CARD schedules the full MoE layer in one framework, covering token dispatch, expert computation, the return of expert outputs, shared experts, and the backward pass. This reduces communication exposed on the critical path.

  2. 02

    Expert computation

    Streamline expert computation and backpropagation

    For grouped matrix multiplication, activations, and routing probability calculations inside MoE experts, CARD reduces the intermediate tensors saved by standard autograd and its backward scheduling overhead. Selected paths use direct gradient computation and accumulation to reduce redundant computation and device memory use.

  3. 03

    Output & loss

    Optimize the output layer and loss

    For the LM head and loss, CARD combines chunked loss computation, output-layer gradient accumulation, and matrix accumulation on the NPU. Together, they preserve the loss result and reduce intermediate buffers and repeated matrix multiplications in the backward pass of large matrix operations.

  4. 04

    Pipeline & memory

    Balance memory and recomputation

    CARD chooses activation storage and recomputation policies for each pipeline stage to balance memory use and recomputation. Automatic policy generation reduces manual configuration and makes the policies easier to adapt to different layer counts and parallelism configurations.
